The data in this map comprises estimated annual emissions of all motorized vehicles on the road [t per road-km per year].
Emissions are calculated based on the estimated daily traffic volume, speed-dependent fuel consumption, and fuel emission factors.
The emission calculation accounts for the vehicle stock shares of different vehicle types and fuel types in Germany.

Note: Minor roads (residential roads, living streets, and unclassified roads) are excluded from the traffic emission estimation
due to the lack of traffic count data for these road classes.

Under "Emission charts" tag in the computation results, you can find the mean annual CO emissions
in t/km² and t/road-km, summarized per city district.

Note: The emission charts only appear in your computation if the selected area has administrative sub-areas
(e.g. city districts) that can be compared to each other.
